# Digital Research Toolkit for Linguists

Author: `anna.pryslopska[ AT ]ling.uni-stuttgart.de`

These are the original materials from the course "Digital Research Toolkit for Linguists taught by me in the Summer Semester 2025 at the University of Stuttgart.

If you want to replicate this course, you can do so with proper attribution. To replicate the data, follow [this link for the experiment](https://farm.pcibex.net/r/CuZHnp/) (full Moses illusion experiment).
